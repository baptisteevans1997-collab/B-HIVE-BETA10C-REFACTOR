07 Operating System

A small weekly rhythm that holds.

Light enough to actually run. The aim isn't more grind, it's turning insight into a system you run on, so focus stops being a mood you have to summon.

Check daily

  • One priority task done before noon, the hard one, first.
  • Touch only the current finish-first project. Other ideas go to the parking list.
  • End-of-day, one question: did I ship something, or only polish?
  • Capture stray ideas in writing, capture is not permission to start.

Check weekly

  • Review the open-loop list, close or formally kill at least one.
  • Look at real numbers on the main project: sessions, sales, results, not design.
  • Take one concrete action toward the next-path decision.
  • Ship one visible thing publicly, small counts.
  • Name next week's single priority before the week starts.

Stop doing

  • Opening new preview files for work that's already good enough.
  • Starting a new project before closing or killing an open one.
  • Redesigning things that already work.
  • Seeking opinions before a real first draft exists.
  • Measuring progress in polish instead of in shipped outcomes.

Protect

  • Deep-work mornings, no admin, no inputs, one task.
  • The day-job income, it funds the dream; do not quit it in frustration.
  • Your taste, don't let speed-to-ship erase the standard.
  • Rest and training, the body, the engine the whole map runs on.
  • Time for the people in your corner.

11 Field Manual

How to work the hive.

Every system in here, and how to use it. Skim it once; come back when something new unlocks.

Getting around

Screens

The app is a stack of screens, not a long scroll. Flip between them with the ❮ ❯ arrows in the bottom bar or the menu button. The bar always names where you are.

The hive

Reading the hive

Your life drawn as 14 cells in a honeycomb. A cell's frame size is how much that area matters to you. Its glowing core is how much energy is actually going in. A big hollow frame is something important going hungry; a small cell glowing past its edge is energy leaking somewhere minor.

Open a cell

Tap any hexagon to open it — what the hive knows about that area, the gap between how much it matters and how much it gets, and what to do about it.

The four reads

Every cell lands in one of four states — drained, overclocked, optimal or standby. The cards under the hive sort your cells into those four, so the pattern is obvious at a glance.

Workers & learning

Workers

The little bees in each cell are what the hive has learned about you there. The better it knows that part of your life, the bigger the swarm.

Teaching the hive

Open a cell and teach it something true, or answer the short question it asks you. Every answer becomes a new worker — this is how the hive gets sharper.

Levels & Agents

Cells gain levels as they learn. At cell level 3 a cell evolves into an Agent — a sharper form that can act on that area, not just hold facts about it. The whole hive levels up alongside them.

Evolution

The Evolution panel traces every bee bloodline up its branch from the hive's root. Teaching a cell pushes its bees further up.

The bees

Each caste moves differently

Every cell is worked by one of six castes, and each has its own tempo. Nurses stay close and careful. Builders work the comb edge. Foragers ferry outward and back. Guards orbit slow on a wide perimeter. Scouts roam fast and far. Drones drift low and lazy.

Cell state shows in the motion

A drained cell's bees move sluggishly. A leaking cell's bees buzz harder. A dormant cell barely twitches. An aligned cell hums calmly. Step a cell's energy up or down and you'll see its swarm change pace within a beat.

Envoys gather to the Queen

Tap the Core and one bee from each cell breaks orbit and drifts in to listen. Tap a normal cell and they ease back to their own swarm.

The activity readout

Each cell's panel says what its bees are doing right now in plain English, like "The Builders are shipping fresh comb" or "The Scouts are scouting cold, faint signals". It reads from the caste and the cell's current state.

Today in the hive

The top of Hive Mind is your daily compass: days since your last check-in, what changed, and one suggested Next move. The recommended cell gets a small pulsing gold dot.
